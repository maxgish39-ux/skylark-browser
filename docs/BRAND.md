# Skylark brand

- **Name:** Skylark. A lark flies high and sings in the open sky.
- **Mark:** a white lark in flight with a forked tail and a raised wing, a small amber sun behind it, on an indigo-to-cyan rounded square.
- **Colors:** indigo #4F46E5, cyan #22D3EE, sun #FFC83D, ink #14161F (dark theme background #0E1018).
- **Tagline:** Browse the open web.
- **Files:** `skylark-logo.svg` (source), `skylark-icon-512.png`, `skylark-banner.png`. Android: `ic_logo_mark.xml` (in-app), `ic_launcher_*.xml` (adaptive icon with a themed monochrome layer), `ic_bird.xml` (error page).
